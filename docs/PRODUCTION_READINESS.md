# Production readiness and acceptance

This release supports one Node app replica. `APP_REPLICA_COUNT` must remain `1`; startup rejects other values. Live speech decoders, calls, audio cache, Salesforce and HubSpot pending reviews, and rate limits use process memory. A Redis migration must store serializable session state, use owner and session-version checks, atomic compare-and-delete for reviews, distributed rate limits, and a durable work queue. Pending review closures need to become persisted commands with idempotency keys before replicas can safely share them. Test two replicas with requests alternating between them, concurrent confirmations, disconnects, expiry, and restart recovery before changing the guard.

HubSpot OAuth is implemented, but account scopes must be granted in the provider dashboard. Use a dedicated sandbox account to validate contacts, companies, deals, tickets, and the exact objects needed. Salesforce and HubSpot reviewed create/edit/delete acceptance must use labelled sandbox records, verify read-back, check audit events, and clean up those records. No sandbox account is currently available; do not use customer records as fixtures. The product treats the local CRM as primary and Twenty, HubSpot, and Salesforce as optional adapters.

For a public deployment, supply a trusted HTTPS domain and certificate, managed secrets, private database/voice/LLM networks, centralized logs without transcripts or tokens, alerts on readiness and error rates, scheduled backup verification, and a restore rehearsal. Run retention on a schedule and verify it against disposable recordings. Test sustained load with representative English and Malay audio and document p50/p95 latency, rejection rates, and capacity. Review the deployed network, identity, recording access, OAuth callbacks, and backup permissions in a deployment-specific threat model. These are deployment tasks; local Compose health alone does not establish them.

Speech evaluation requires corrected, consented recordings in a private manifest with language, accent and noise labels. `scripts/evaluate_speech.py` reports corpus word and character error rates, entity accuracy, p50/p95 latency, and cohort success rates. Keep the audio and reports out of Git. The planner evaluation in `scripts/evaluate.ts` measures intent and write proposal safety on fictional cases; expand it with held-out English and Malay examples, false-write proposals, and ambiguous contacts before setting release thresholds. Do not claim an accuracy target until a representative held-out dataset exists.
